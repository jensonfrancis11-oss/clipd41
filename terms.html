CLIP.D41

A personal tool that turns popular public gaming stream clips into short vertical videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

Terms of Service

Clip.D41 is a private, single-user tool run by its owner. It is not offered to the public and there is no sign-up.

What it does

  • Finds public clips from live-streaming sites, edits them into 9:16 videos with captions, and credits the original streamer and clip link in every post.
  • Every video is checked by the owner before it is posted.
  • Uploads only to accounts the owner has connected and authorised.

Content and takedowns

If you are a streamer and want a clip of yours removed or never used, contact the owner through the Clip.D41 YouTube or TikTok profile and it will be taken down and your channel excluded.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is. The owner may change or stop it at any time.
