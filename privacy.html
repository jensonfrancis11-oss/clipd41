CLIP.D41

A personal tool that turns popular public gaming stream clips into short vertical videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

Last updated 9 November 2026

Privacy Policy

Clip.D41 connects only to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

What it accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile info (display name, open ID) to confirm the connected account, and permission to upload videos to that account.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's channel.

What it stores

Access tokens are kept on the owner's own computer only, used solely to upload videos, and never sold or shared. No data about other TikTok or YouTube users is collected.

Removing access

The connection can be revoked at any time from TikTok settings (Security, Manage app permissions) or from your Google account's third-party access page.
